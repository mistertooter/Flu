🤧 How Humans Catch The Flu

1. Exposure

Virus droplets enter nearby air.

2. Inhalation

Droplets enter through nose and mouth.

3. Airway Entry

Particles move through the respiratory tract.

4. Lung Infection

Virus reaches bronchi and alveoli.

5. Symptoms

Infection spreads and symptoms develop.
